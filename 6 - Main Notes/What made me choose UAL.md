## What interests me about this opportunity


My research interests lie in additive manufacturing as a design bedrock rather than just a fabrication tool. I am interested in how this can be used alongside exotic materials and geometries to create products that cannot be created through traditional means. E.g. shock absorbent materials to manufacture shoes/shock absorbers in one piece   

I am also drawn to use existing electronics in new  form, where design can extend the life and functionality of devices. E.g. Converting a laptop to a handheld.

I am also interested in improving the 3D printer itself through hardware and software mods. E.g. The OrcaSlicer project.

I want to explore how emerging manufacturing techniques can reshape both the objects we create and the processes through which we create them.
